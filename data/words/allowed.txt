# Names that are valid in an identifier but are not in english.txt: brands,
# obscure coding terms, and the one-letter or digit-bearing pieces (utf8, json,
# wayland, ttf). One lowercase piece per line, no spaces. A line starting with
# '#' and a blank line are comments. A name is split on '_' and every piece must
# be in english.txt or here (idc --word-scan; docs/ERRORS.md, E0311).
